## 1. The question

Does explicitly supervising **Gamma** (with Vega) inside a trained, PDE-retaining, parametric
Heston PINN improve **delta-only** hedging PnL under **misspecified** dynamics — and through
which channel: transaction-cost/turnover, or model-uncertainty robustness?

Three design choices make this a causal study rather than a benchmark:

1. **Supervision ladder.** price → +Δ → +Δ+Γ → +Δ+Γ+ν. The claim lives specifically on the
   **rung1→rung2 gap** — the marginal effect of adding *second-order* supervision to a model that
   already has first-order supervision. This is the rung that Noguer's differential-ML result
   says should be insufficient for held-out Gamma.
2. **Label-noise dose-response.** Gamma labels corrupted at σ ∈ {0, 10%, 25%, 50%}, plus a
   shuffled endpoint and a Black-Scholes cheap-label point. Monotone ⇒ label *correctness*
   matters; flat ⇒ the effect is regularization, not information.
3. **Adjudicated mechanism.** A pre-registered 2×2 and a transaction-cost sweep {0, 1, 2%} with
   an excess-turnover statistic `T_ex`. The cost channel is *falsifiable*: if `T_ex` is unmoved,
   the cost explanation dies regardless of what the PnL shows. **No outcome is pre-committed.**

All thresholds were fixed in `heston_benchmark_v6.yaml` before any result existed. Every
pre-registered failure has a stated, publishable null reading.

## 3. The only numbers that exist today

I am listing these explicitly so there is no ambiguity about what has and has not been measured.
**None of them is a result of the study.**

**(a) Oracle trust gates — passing.** The 4-leg oracle reproduces Albrecher (2007) Table 1 prices,
collapses to Black-Scholes as ξ→0 for all Greeks, agrees across three legs within `tol_rel = 1e-3`
(0 of 15 baseline points masked), and is bit-reproducible from seed. The pre-freeze certification
(`oracle.py --full`) has not been run.

**(b) A pilot null on the UDE arm.** A scratchpad-scale pilot (200×16 labels, 6 000 steps, 5 seeds
— roughly 3% of production fidelity) put the UDE arm at misspecified delta CVaR95 = 9.72 vs
`standard_pinn` = 9.56 at the confirmatory cell: **~1.7% worse**, paired-bootstrap CI on the worse
side, stable across all three cost tiers. Against the ≥10% pre-registered threshold this is an
**honest null**. It is a pilot at a fidelity far below production; it constrains nothing about the
main claim, and the UDE arm is scoped in the contract as *one comparison, one number, one
sentence*.

## 4. Correctness audit

Before spending compute I ran a **read-only** adversarial audit of the pipeline, targeting the
class of bug that does not crash: the one that produces a plausible, confident, *wrong* verdict.
No source file was modified. Every P1 claim is backed by a reproduction script that was actually
executed, with captured output (`audit/repro/`, 8 scripts).

**Outcome: 0 P0 · 5 P1 · 7 P2 · 2 P3.** No live numerical mismatch was found. The one class that
could have produced one — contract constants re-typed as Python literals — I diffed exhaustively:
**19 duplicates, 0 drifted**.

The three to fix before any compute is spent:

| ID | Problem | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **A2** | The mechanism classifier is **sign-blind**. An arm that is significantly *worse* at 0% TC is labelled `channel_i` — the robustness channel — identically to one that is significantly better. | This is the only finding that can produce a **confident, wrong, affirmative claim**. The pre-registered null would be reported as a mechanism *finding*, and it propagates verbatim into the verdict table and the E2 figure. Two comparison operators fix it. |
| **G1** | The headroom gate's 10% decision threshold is hardcoded rather than read from the contract. | The value is currently correct, so this is insurance, not a repair — but the gate is the go/no-go on the entire compute budget, and it runs first. Cheapest possible insurance on the most expensive decision. |
| **I1** | A plateau in the information-matching curve caused by the **training-row cap** is indistinguishable from a genuine information plateau. | If the frozen label artifact holds fewer than 5N rows, the top rungs train on bit-identical data, the curve is flat by construction, and `plateau_reached=True` is reported. Must be fixed *before* the artifact is frozen, because the artifact size decides whether it fires. |

The remaining P1s (A1, A3, C1) are threshold-provenance and seed-count guards; A3 in particular
prevents the confirmatory verdict from being declared on a partial seed set. The P2/P3 items are
diagnostic or numerically inert — the largest is a 0.37% discrepancy between the realized
rebalance grid (`dt = 0.003953`) and the contract's declared `dt = 0.003968`, which needs a
one-line disclosure or a contract edit, your call.

**What the audit found clean, by execution rather than inspection:** identical price-head
initialization across all 19 arms; the UDE correction exactly zero at initialization with a
bit-identical PDE residual; loss scales identical across arms; disabled loss terms genuinely
absent; provider chunking bit-exact; common random numbers genuinely common; transaction-cost
accounting and the exact PnL decomposition balancing at every cost tier; and both Tier-1 entry
points bit-identical across fresh processes.

**Honest limitation.** This audit is entirely *pre-compute*. The bug classes that survive it are
the ones that only appear against fitted models and frozen artifacts — a checkpoint loaded from
the wrong arm, a mask that turns out non-neutral, a Greek that is well-behaved at initialization
and pathological after 20 000 steps. I found no evidence of any of those, but none of those
artifacts exist yet to inspect, so absence of evidence is weak here.

## 5. Critical path

1. **Apply the three audit fixes** (A2, G1, I1) — hours, no compute.
2. **Full oracle certification** (`oracle.py --full`) — the pre-freeze gate.
3. **Freeze the label and dataset artifacts** — needs approval; `--n-points` must be chosen large
   enough that the I1 row cap cannot bind.
4. **Pilot fit → oracle-headroom gate.** This is the real decision point. The gate measures the
   maximum detectable effect by hedging the confirmatory instrument with oracle Greeks vs oracle
   Greeks + calibrated noise. **If the spread falls below the pre-registered 10% CVaR95 threshold,
   the contract requires RETUNE** — rebalancing frequency or misspecification severity — *before*
   any training. This is non-optional and is the honest answer to Sakuma's Table 5 power warning
   (oracle ≈ network hedge), which is the single largest threat to the study.
5. **Training grid** — 80 runs (19 arms; 10 seeds on the three confirmatory arms, 5 elsewhere),
   ≈33 GPU-hours, **≈$65** at L40S list price. Dispatch is built and defaults to dry-run.
6. **Evaluation → analysis → exhibits.** Fully automated once checkpoints exist; the analysis layer
   emits the pre-registered verdicts directly, and E1–E4 are pure functions of the frozen CSVs.
